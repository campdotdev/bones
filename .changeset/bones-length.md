`data-bones-length="9"` makes a bar nine characters wide, measured in the element's own font. Values 1 through 40 work in every supported browser. Any other integer works where the browser supports `attr()` with a type. A length is a modifier, not a type: an inferred leaf with a length is still inferred, and outside a busy region the attribute is inert. The stylesheet is 5.7 kB gzipped, up from 4.5.
